Pannangala Panthers

Our Story

A brotherhood forged on the hockey fields of Kodagu. Passionate players, proud traditions, and a tournament that changed the game forever.

Est. 2012

Rooted in Kodagu

Pannangala Panthers was born in the heart of Yavakapadi, Kodagu, from a simple love for field hockey. What began as a handful of friends chasing a ball on village mud has grown into one of the most respected amateur clubs in the district.

We believe hockey is more than a sport here — it is discipline, community, and a way of life passed from one generation to the next.

The Tournament We Started

Kailpoudh Namme

The Beginning

Pannangala Panthers proudly started the Kailpoudh Namme Hockey Tournament, bringing together clubs from across Kodagu — and soon beyond — to celebrate the district's deep hockey heritage.

A Festival of Hockey

Kailpoudh Namme is more than a competition. It is a festival where villages, families, and old rivals meet on the pitch, sharing fierce matches and stronger friendships.

Legacy in Coorg

Year after year, the tournament has become a landmark event in Coorg's sporting calendar, inspiring young players and keeping Kodagu's hockey spirit alive.
